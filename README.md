# Mudoven Cafe (मड ओवन) — Ultra-Luxury Google Review & Instagram QR System

A high-luxury hospitality QR landing page and 300 DPI printable table standee suite crafted for **Mudoven Cafe (`@mudoven_indore`)**, modeled after the Shree Kripa luxury standee & landing page architecture and custom-tailored to Mudoven's iconic night glass facade, golden atrium (`PIZZA TODAY SALAD TOMORROW • WE SPEAK THE GOOD FOOD LANGUAGE`), and signature orange & 24k gold brand identity.

## 🔗 Configured Destination Links
- **Rate Us on Google:** `https://www.google.com/gasearch?q=mudoven%20reviews&source=sh/x/gs/m2/5#ebo=3`
- **Follow Us on Instagram:** `https://www.instagram.com/mudoven_indore?stkn=MTl0c2Q4eWVueXkwNg==` (`@mudoven_indore`)

## 📁 Key Files
- `index.html` — Ultra-luxury mobile QR landing page with 24k Gold Rim `M Cafe` medallion, transparent `Mudoven — Your Forever Happy Place (Est. 2012)` wordmark, interactive Google & Instagram cards, architectural showcase strip, and one-tap call button.
- `standee.html` — Interactive 300 DPI Printable Standee Studio with 4 modes (Smart Hub QR, Dual Direct QR, Google Direct QR, Instagram Direct QR).
- `config.js` — Central configuration file for links, phone number, and brand text.
- `generate_qr.py` — Automated Python generator that processes the uploaded photos/logos and outputs 300 DPI printable standees and QR codes:
  - `table_standee_printable.png` / `standee_front_printable.png`
  - `standee_dual_direct_static.png`
  - `standee_google_direct.png`
  - `standee_instagram_direct.png`
  - `mobile_landing_preview.png`
